# x. `x_silent_review_hold`

**What happened:** A new-feature run handed its draft pull request over for review. The review job skipped the draft ("reviewed when it is marked ready") on a path that logged nothing, and its ending was dropped because nothing had marked the job queued: no verdict, no record, no status. The ready gate kept the draft until CoderHelm's review approved the head, so each waited on the other and the pull request was held on every sweep with CI green. Its four held items were CoderHelm's own sandbox: Node switched to the repository's version had no pnpm, and every step exited 127. Another pull request lost its review the same night to the claim and dedup key of a push review CoderHelm had skipped as its own push.

**The guarantee:** Every review job ends in one visible outcome (a verdict, a skip with its reason, a retry or a failure): one `review_job_ended` line and a live status, created when none exists, and every early return names its reason. A run's hand-off reviews its pull request, draft or not; a held head claim makes it wait and look again, never skip; a skipped push review gives its claim and dedup key back. When the draft decision needs CoderHelm's approval and no review of the head exists, the gate queues one once per head and waits ("CoderHelm is reviewing this head"), and the verdict asks for the decision again; a reviewer that is off is named. A sandbox step that exited 127 or printed a tooling error, and every step after such an install, is CoderHelm's tooling and never counts against the change; the sandbox makes the root's package manager available first.
