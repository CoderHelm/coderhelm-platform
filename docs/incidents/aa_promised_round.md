# aa. `aa_promised_round`

**What happened:** A ticket run's own review requested changes. The run took review fix rounds until its automatic fix budget was spent, handed its pull request over with two of its own review's findings still listed, and finished. The draft was then held every hour for three days on "CoderHelm fixes them in its review rounds", and no round ever ran after the run was done.

**The guarantee:** A hold never promises CoderHelm work that isn't queued. A finished run's draft held on its own review's findings queues one review fix round for them, once per head, while the run has rounds left (its own review rounds, its automatic fix pushes and its fix cycles), and waits for it ("CoderHelm is fixing N findings from its review"), a wait bounded like every other. With no round left, after the run stopped its rounds itself, or once a round ran on the head and the findings are still there, the hold names what a person does and promises nothing. The security and planned work holds name a person's action too, since nothing runs those again once the run is over. A review of a finished run's pull request leaves its findings to that decision, so they are never fixed twice or outside the run's budget.
