# ao. `ao_stopped_log_finding`

**What happened:** Two log findings of one production service stayed on the Logs page as Critical and New, each with "Create fix PR" and "Not started automatically: not sure the repo is ...; check it and start the fix", more than a day after their error had stopped: hundreds of matching lines in a few hours, then none while the service wrote its usual volume. The team had fixed the cause the day before. Only time closed a finding (three days without a line), so a problem that was over read as a live emergency that needed a person.

**The guarantee:** Every analysis counts each open finding's own lines on its own log group and keeps the count with the lines the group wrote. Two analyses in a row with no matching line at the service's usual volume make the finding stopped: shown and counted as stopped, never New or a live critical, with no automatic fix and no stale "choose a repo" reason. When the service itself went quiet nobody can tell, and it stays as it was. A burst that stopped after a change in its repo resolves after one day instead of three, with that evidence in the reason; an error that keeps coming back keeps its longer period. Seen again, it is live again and its fix may start. Evidence for the repo that couldn't be weighed is weighed again next sweep and never put to a person.
