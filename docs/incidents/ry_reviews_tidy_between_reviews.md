# ry. `ry_reviews_tidy_between_reviews`

**What happened:** A person reading two pull requests on a web app asked whether CoderHelm's approvals and resolved comments could go away after a while, because there was too much of them to read. On the first, CoderHelm's "Approved with notes" review from days earlier still stood in full: the file table, the "Why approved" line, a risk block, an FYI list, Verification, Tests, "Earlier comments" and the rating footer. A person's "Address the review" commit and a second review just as long sat under it. On the second, an inline thread of CoderHelm's from the day before was still open: "Blocking (failing check): Lint & Type Check and Deploy Preview are red on this head … I could not read the logs". It was about an older head whose checks had passed since. CoderHelm only cleaned up its earlier reviews when it posted a new one. A push, checks going green, code leaving the diff and a merge all left everything as it was.

**The guarantee:** CoderHelm's own reviews and threads are tidied on a push, when checks finish, on a merge or a close, and on the review sweep. A review body for an older commit than the head becomes one line, for example "✅ Approved `abc1234` (superseded by later commits)". The exception is a request for changes, which still holds the merge and keeps its reasons until the next review. On a merge or a close every body becomes one line and CoderHelm's resolved threads are hidden. A thread about failing checks resolves once the head's checks pass, and so does a thread whose file or lines left the diff (a required-rule thread is not resolved just because its lines moved). CoderHelm's comments in those threads are hidden. A new review keeps only its verdict, its reason and what holds the merge in sight; the overview, file table, risk, FYI, verification, tests and earlier comments go under one collapsed block. A person's review, a thread a person opened and an unanswered reply are never touched, and nothing is posted to announce the cleanup.

**Where it's enforced:** `common::review_supersede` (`tidy`, `settled`, `checks_pass`, `fold`), carried out by `worker::passes::review_tidy` (the `review_tidy` message and the review sweep) and queued by `gateway::routes::github_webhook` (`queue_review_tidy` on `synchronize`, `closed` and `check_suite` `completed`). The body is folded in `review_pr`.
