A team that already runs on hosted CoderHelm (app.coderhelm.com) can move to your own install and keep its settings, history and connections. CoderHelm arranges the move with you; nothing is copied until both sides confirm.

## Ask for the move

1. Install CoderHelm with the [install guide](https://docs.coderhelm.com/self-host/install/). Its second step, **Your data**, offers **Start fresh** or **Move my team from CoderHelm (hosted)**.
2. Choose **Move my team** and press **Ask CoderHelm to move my team**. A page opens on app.coderhelm.com with your install's address, AWS region and name filled in.
3. Sign in as an **admin or owner of the team to move**. The request is for the team you are signed in to; to move a different one, switch to it in the team menu first. Members can't ask.
4. Add a good time and any notes, and press **Ask to move this team**. You get a ticket number and these next steps. The guide doesn't wait: press **Continue** and finish the install.

Already installed? Open the same page from Support on app.coderhelm.com.

Never put AWS keys, tokens or passwords in the request. CoderHelm never needs them to move your team, and a request that contains one is refused.

## What happens next

1. Someone from CoderHelm answers on the ticket, usually within a working day.
2. You agree on a time, and both sides confirm the team and the install.
3. Your team's settings, history and connections are copied to your install and checked, count by count.
4. Hosted CoderHelm keeps working the whole time. You switch when you are ready.
5. People sign in to your install with the same email and password.

After the switch you link GitHub and Jira again from your install's Settings, and CoderHelm removes the team from hosted only when you ask.

## Limits

- One move request per team per day; reply on the open ticket for anything more.
- Only the team you are signed in to. Several teams? Ask from each one.
