# Brand voice

This is a template. Replace it with your own voice in your copy of the
plugin, or point the `brand_voice_doc` setting at your document.

- Lead with what changed for the reader, not with the company.
- Short sentences. Plain words. One idea per paragraph.
- Say what the feature does, then why it matters, then where to try it.
- No superlatives you can't back up, no exclamation marks, no emoji in
  emails.
- Name the product the way the `product_name` setting spells it.

## Channels

- **LinkedIn:** under 1,300 characters; the first line works on its own.
  Write three variants with different openings so an editor can pick.
- **Blog:** a title, a two sentence summary, then sections with headings.
- **Email:** a subject under 60 characters, a preview line, three short
  paragraphs and one link.

See `examples.md` next to this skill for the shape of each.
